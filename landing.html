FAQ
Some seasons are already underway. How does that work?

Each league counts one season. If it's already going (NFL, college football, the Premier League), this season counts, games already played included. Points come from where a team finishes, so you just get to draft with a few weeks of results to go on.

If a league is in its playoffs or done (MLB, WNBA), you're drafting for its next season. The app still shows this year's results, marked as not counting. Golf counts the FedEx Cup season that starts in January.

Do I set lineups each week?

Nope. Once the draft is done, there's nothing to manage. You just cheer your teams on through the end of their seasons, and every team you drafted earns you points.

How does the draft work?

Everyone drafts together, live in Boxscore, from a phone, iPad or computer. It's a snake draft: the order flips each round, so whoever picks last in one round picks first in the next.

Can't stay for the whole thing? Auto draft will make your picks for you. Want to practice first? Run a Mock Draft any time before the real one.

When is the draft?

Once the group is full, the commissioner posts a poll in the app with a few possible times. Everyone marks the ones they can make, and the time that works best becomes the draft. The app shows it on Home and can send you an alert.

Does it cost anything?

This year's buy-in is $20 per person, and you can pay it at the end of the season.
