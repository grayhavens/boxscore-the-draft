FAQ
Which sports do we play?

Your commissioner chooses from the leagues Boxscore follows, and sets how many teams everyone drafts in each, from 1 to 5. A league can also be scores only: it shows in the app, but nobody drafts it.

Do I set lineups each week?

Nope. Once the draft is done, there's nothing to manage: no lineups, trades or waivers. Your teams earn points from how their seasons go, like playoff runs, titles and top finishes, and lose some for finishing last. The app keeps score for you.

How does the draft work?

One draft covers every league: each round, you can take a team from any sport. Everyone drafts together, live in Boxscore, from a phone, iPad or computer. It's a snake draft: the order flips each round, so whoever picks last in one round picks first in the next.

Can't stay for the whole thing? Auto draft will make your picks for you. Want to practice first? Run a Mock Draft any time before the real one.

When in the year should we draft?

Any time. Each league counts the season that's underway when you draft, games already played included. If a league is already in its playoffs or finished, its next season counts instead.

The best time is early September, right as the NFL and college football seasons kick off. Nearly every league is just starting or still to come, so your group gets the whole year together.

How do we pick a draft time?

Once the group is full, the commissioner posts a poll in the app with a few possible times. Everyone marks the ones they can make, and the time that works best becomes the draft. The app shows it on Home and can send you an alert.

Is this a mobile app?

It's a web app, so there's nothing to download from an app store. It's built for your phone first, and it works on an iPad or computer too.

Add it to your Home Screen and it opens full screen with its own icon, just like an app.
