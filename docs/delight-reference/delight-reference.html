Delight reference

Design references for docs/delight-plan.md: 01 draft clock, 03 since last night, 04 champion, 05 chat gestures, 06 team page, 08 landing app tour. Every phone is interactive; Replay resets it. Serve over HTTP, don’t open from file://.

01

Draft room: on the clock

The clock is the room’s heartbeat. It turns red and pulses at 8 seconds. When your pick lands, the team’s color floods the hero, the badge flies home and the snake rail moves on.

03

Since last night

The first open of the day starts with a short stack of what changed overnight. Swipe each card away or clear them all, then Home cascades in. It shows once a day, not on every open.

04

Champion crowned

The season gets one loud moment. Letters rise, the total counts up, then gold and team-color confetti. It ends in a share card sized for the group chat.

05

Chat gestures

Hold a message and slide across the bar; the emoji under your finger grows. Let go and it flies onto the message. Swipe right to reply. Gestures replace menus.

06

Team page

The team’s color carries the hero and tints the bar as it collapses on scroll. Swipe the hero sideways to move through your teams; pull it down and it stretches. “Path to points” shows what the team is worth to you, and what it still could be.

08

Landing: app tour

Same landing, with the explainer rebuilt as a scroll tour of the real app. A mini screen sticks in place and scrolling plays through the draft room, team page, Scores, Chat and Points. Each scene is a pure function of scroll progress, so it scrubs both ways. The tab pill springs to each page, and the segments jump between steps.
